## 6. The strategist point: why this fault is survivable, and what that says about the design

The previous tick's fault-#10 framing stands, and today sharpens it in a direction that is *favourable*
and worth recording because the programme's instinct is to record only the damage.

**The pure-python engine layer is why a dead thinking-agent shell is not a dead programme.**
`durable_tick.sh` runs `basket_forward session`, `newsdesk resolve` and `predmarket resolve` as
self-guarded subshells *before* Claude is invoked, and they ran fine today. The consequence is precise
and load-bearing given the calendar:

> **The PREDMARKET first settlement on 2026-09-06 is SAFE even if the agent's shell never comes back.**
> T43 deliberately wired `resolve_pass()` to run first inside `pull`, but the tick-level
> `predmarket_resolve` engine is a *second, independent* path that does not depend on the agent
> executing anything. Harvesting is protected. What is lost is only *proposing* and the T47 coherence
> screen — i.e. the discretionary half, which has abstained every tick since 08-31 anyway.

The general rule this earns: **work that must happen on a clock belongs in a self-guarded engine, not in
an agent's tick.** Every capability that survived today is one that was written as a program; every one
that died is one that was written as an instruction to an agent. CHUNK C and CHUNK D are pure
instruction, which is why they are simply unavailable to two consecutive ticks. That is an argument for
keeping the agent on judgement and the clocks on cron — which is what the converged direction already
assumes, and today is the first real test of it.
