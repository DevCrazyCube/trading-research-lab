01:45 today), and the disk was already near-full from long-standing data (orphanflush_cache 39G —

2. **Copytruncate of `raw_frames.jsonl`** (the logrotate pattern): sealed the full content into
   zstd segments `raw_frames.seg1_20260901T1223.jsonl.zst` + `raw_frames.seg2_20260901T1223.jsonl.zst`

   persistent `open(path, "a")` O_APPEND handle, so its next write lands at the new EOF. Loss is

   reported byte-exact in FINDINGS. **No process was killed, no arm state touched, no decoded
   evidence (`feed_events.jsonl`, ledgers, seals) modified.**
