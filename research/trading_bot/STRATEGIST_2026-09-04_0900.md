**(a) T49 → PRIORITY 1**, level with T50 and ahead of T48. Rationale stated so it can be audited: this is
not a judgement that T49 became more valuable, it is a judgement that **T50 Step 3 and T48 tranche 6

budget, not its remainder, and this tick's budget went to closing the U4 / `results_unlocked` gap and to
the queue decision above. That is the last tick for which that is an acceptable answer.

verification whose subject cannot have changed since the previous reading is not evidence, it is padding
wearing the costume of diligence. Concretely: **T31 is verified on a tick after ~02:30 CEST; T30, T33 and

the T50 standing rule — an unverified ledger must still be reported as NOT VERIFIED — it only stops the
programme from paying full price three times for one fact.
