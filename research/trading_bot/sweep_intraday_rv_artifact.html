The spread ate the signal

Intraday relative value and mean reversion across ETF siblings, sector baskets and the mega-cap cross-section. The signal is real, large, and it replicates out of sample. It is also, to within a factor of thirty, entirely inside the bid–ask.

Verdict · theme is dead

98 structurally different formulas were scored on the design window. Zero are net-positive at the headline 1.0 bp/side cost rung. The best is D1_gap_rev_mega at -0.27, against a noise bar — the 95th percentile of best-of-98 Sharpe when nothing has an edge — of +2.01. The winner is not near the bar. It is 2.3 Sharpe units below it.

The interesting part is why, and it is not “there is no signal”:

  • Before costs the signal is real and it replicates. 50 of 98 configs beat the gross noise bar (+1.59). The best gross design Sharpe is +6.86 (A2_pair_z_intraday), and the same formula prints +5.41 gross on the untouched held-out window.
  • The edge is smaller than the friction by one to two orders of magnitude. A2_pair_z_intraday can bear a total charge of 0.031 bp per unit of turnover before its gross edge is exactly consumed. The headline rung charges 1.15 bp.
  • It cannot pay the regulatory fee, never mind the spread. SEC Section 31 plus FINRA TAF alone is 0.15 bp per unit of turnover — five times what the best formula earns.
  • Only 3 of 98 configs are positive even at the 0.5 bp/side rung, and the best of those reaches +0.22 — still far under the noise bar.

Mean reversion between ETF siblings and across the short-horizon equity cross-section is not a mispricing a taker can harvest. It is, to a very good approximation, the bid–ask bounce itself. The thing being measured is the spread, which is exactly why the measurement is so stable and the trade is impossible.

Method

Design window
2021-01-04 → 2024-12-31 · 1005 sessions
Held out
2025-01-02 → 2026-08-15 · 405 sessions, not read until the shortlist was frozen. The sweep script refuses to run the held-out stage without an explicit shortlist, so it cannot be used to browse.
Universe
132 symbols of Alpaca SIP 5-minute bars, regular hours only, split- and dividend-adjusted. Same-claim ETF clusters, SPDR/Vanguard sector twins, style and regional ETFs, levered and inverse funds, 30+ mega-caps. Survivorship-biased — every ETF is alive today — and price levels are not point-in-time, so no config filters on a level.
Execution
Signal from closes through bar t−1; fill at the VWAP of bar t; return vwap[t+1]/vwap[t]−1. A full five-minute bar separates the last observed price from the fill, and the fill is a bar VWAP rather than a touch print. Flat at every close.
Costs
0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.5 bp per side on |Δw|, headline 1.0 bp, plus 0.15 bp per unit turnover for SEC 31 and FINRA TAF on every rung. Provenance is an adverse proxy: the programme’s own measured half-spread for liquid names is 1.13 bp, and 2.5 bp is the repo’s retail-taker default. Borrow is zero, and that is a claim with a reason — every config is flat at the closing bar and US borrow accrues overnight.
Noise bar
Stationary block bootstrap of demeaned daily PnL, same day indices across all configs so cross-config correlation is preserved, max Sharpe per resample, 500 resamples.
Harness self-test
Recovers a planted OU dislocation at gross Sharpe +12.45, flips to -12.45 under negation, and returns -0.34 on a random-walk spread with nothing to find. All three checks pass, so the evaluator neither misses a real edge nor manufactures one.
Disclosed redundancy
D1_gap_rev_mega and D3_gap_resid_rev_mega return identical numbers — subtracting the cross-sectional mean gap before a dollar-neutralising demean is a no-op. That is one formula counted twice, so the effective N is 97. It moves the noise bar by less than 0.01 and is recorded rather than quietly deduplicated.
