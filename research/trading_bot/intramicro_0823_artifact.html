Intraday microstructure dies on gross, not on cost

Twenty-eight low-turnover intraday formulas — opening drive, order flow, VWAP deviation, opening-range breakout, volume-profile value area, late-day drift. None beat the noise bar on net. None beat it on gross either, and that is the finding.

Verdict — theme is noise
The two bars

The best of 28 sits below the bar before a basis point is charged

The bar is the 95th percentile of the best-of-28 Sharpe under H0 — a stationary block bootstrap (block = 5 sessions, 2,000 resamples) of each config's demeaned daily PnL, drawing the same day indices for every config so the sweep's internal correlation survives. The analytic √(2 ln N)/√years = +1.29 is reported alongside and is not the operative number.

Why this is a harder negative than the last one

The August 20 relative-value sweep found a large, replicable gross edge (design +6.86, held out +5.41) that was unreachable only because of friction — 50 of its 98 configs beat the gross bar. This round changed exactly two things: the holding period went from minutes to hours, and the fill stopped sharing a print with the signal. With those two things removed, the largest gross in 28 tries is +1.34 against a +1.40 bar. The earlier gross number was made of the two things this round took away.

Mechanism

Where the late-day “drift” actually lives

G1_LD500_CONT buys the day's cross-sectional winners at 15:49 across the point-in-time top-500 and exits in the closing auction. On the design window it looks like a modest positive: gross Sharpe +0.55. Decomposing the hold shows that number is not drift.

The gate that caught it

A fill that never shares a print, and a one-bar-gap re-entry test

ρ1(Δspread) is what killed auction-imbalance and manufactured the ETF-sibling leg. On a 5-minute cube the equivalent discipline was frozen in §3 of the pre-registration: a formula observes bars 0..s; the primary fill is the VWAP of bar s+1; the gap fill is the VWAP of bar s+2; the exit at bar 77 is unconditional and therefore carries no signal. A config is disqualified unless its gap Sharpe is at least half its primary Sharpe and positive — however large the primary number is.

The harness self-test proves the gate can see a bounce. A planted signal is recovered at gross +31.85 and mirrors to −32.49 under negation; a random score returns −0.22; and a deliberately planted one-bar artefact — a score that is the next bar's return — scores +7.84 primary and collapses to +1.53 under the gap fill. All four checks pass.

It fired on the one net-positive config too. E4_PDPOC_MAGNET, the previous session's volume-profile point-of-control used as a magnet, goes from +0.21 to −0.03 when the fill moves one bar later — and then prints −0.57 out of sample.

The raw sign of broad late-day drift is not stable across the two halves.

Null 2 · a correction, disclosed

A permutation null on a net series is mis-specified

The cross-sectional null is ADV-slot signal permutation: symbols are bucketed into ADV deciles within each day and the score vector is permuted within buckets, destroying the symbol↔score mapping while preserving the day, the liquidity profile and the score distribution. Rotate-the-returns is broken and is not used.

Run first on the net series, it is meaningless — a permuted book still pays full cost, so the null distribution sits far below zero and any config with non-negative gross beats it. E4 scored p = 0.004 against a null 95th percentile of −0.33. Re-run on gross, which is the question a permutation actually answers:

Four configs carry a per-config p < 0.01. This does not make them real, and saying so is why both nulls get run. These p-values are uncorrected for the 28 draws that produced the ranking; the family-wise bar for that is the best-of-28 bootstrap, and the largest gross in the sweep sits below it. A p-value computed on the winner of a 28-way search is not evidence — and every one of these then fails on cost and out of sample anyway. Note also that the permutation null's mean is positive: the score magnitude profile alone, with the symbol mapping destroyed, already earns something.

What this closes

Four things that no longer need re-testing

  1. Continuous intraday is not different from the auction in the way the theme hoped. The auction family died on ρ1; the continuous late-day family dies on the same object, caught by the gap test instead.
  2. Low turnover removes the friction excuse and reveals there was no gross edge. The median config here loses ~1.2 Sharpe to friction, not ~28 as in the relative-value sweep — the low-turnover design did exactly what it was meant to do to the cost side. It just found nothing to spend the budget on.
  3. Bar-level order-flow proxies are empty. Close-location-value × volume, average trade size versus a name's own norm, volume front-loading, trade-count acceleration: best gross +0.12, best break-even 0.10 bp.
  4. Run cross-sectional permutation nulls on gross, and correct them for the search. Otherwise the null hands you p = 0.004 for a config that is about to print −0.57 out of sample.

Nothing from this theme goes near the basket. The sized basket remains the four legs from the 2026-08-23 v9 scrub — T3, DUALCLASS, T12 CEF discount, ORB — unchanged by this round.

Method

How it was run

Windows. Design 2021-01-04 → 2024-12-31 (1,005 sessions). Held out 2025-01-02 → 2026-08-14 (405 sessions), untouched until the shortlist was committed. Panel-B pre-sample 2016-01-04 → 2020-12-31 (1,259 sessions), declared in advance and barred from promoting anything.

Panel A. Alpaca SIP 5-minute bars, adjustment=all, regular hours, rebuilt into a 1,410 × 78 × 132 cube carrying open, high, low, close, VWAP, dollar volume and trade count. Universes: 34 mega-cap single names, and 49 unlevered non-same-claim ETFs — levered and inverse funds and same-claim siblings are excluded so no config can rediscover the ETF-sibling artefact.

Panel B. The point-in-time top-500-ADV minute panel (1,611 symbols ever, median 497 per day, 2016–2026) with 15:29 / 15:49 / 15:59 prices plus the official open and close. Every Panel-B return is strictly within one session, so its raw unadjusted prices are safe; no overnight return is computed on it.

Weights and cost. Cross-sectionally demeaned, L1-normalised to gross 1.0; turnover 1.88–2.00 per day. Rungs 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.5 bp per side on |Δw|, headline 1.0, plus 0.15 bp per unit turnover for SEC 31 and FINRA TAF. Borrow zero, and that is exact — every config is flat at every close. The closing-auction exit on Panel B is charged 0 bp of spread because M0 measured the official print on 99.96% of top-500 symbol-days, but still pays the fee.

Survivorship. Panel A is survivorship-biased and retroactively adjusted; no config filters on a price level. Panel B is point-in-time in membership and is the honest breadth check on Panel A's survivor set — and it agrees.
