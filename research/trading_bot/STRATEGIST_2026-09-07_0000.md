## THE FINDING: the T30 kill-condition is a scheduled fake-pass on the up side

Found blind, on clean day 14, with no pair-level FEERATE or AVAILABLE figure read by anyone.
Everything below comes from the FROZEN design cache (the pinned 113-pair record), not the
forward panel.

The frozen rule counts PAIRS: *"if <10 of the 113 pairs have both legs borrowable at fee <
that pair's measured gross edge, preferred-series RV is PERMANENTLY DEAD."*

But the 113 pairs are not 113 independent borrow decisions. They are within-issuer
combinations of only **103 legs across 38 issuer roots**:

    PSA 21 pairs (7 series)   AHT 10   BAC 10   MS 10   BML 6   CIM 6
    9 roots x 3 pairs, 23 roots x 1 pair

**PSA alone supplies 21 of the 113 pairs.** One issuer whose preferred ladder happens to be
easy to borrow clears the >=10 threshold by itself. The minimum number of issuer roots that
can alone clear the gate is **1**.

This is the T60 defect shape -- an i.i.d. count over a correlated book -- sitting on the one
gate that drives the pay-or-stop fork. Left alone, a `BORROW_WALL_PASSED` could mean "Public
Storage's preferreds are borrowable", and we would carry that into a spend decision as if it
were ten independent survivors.

Note the asymmetry, which is why this is safe to fix now: clustering can only **inflate** the
alive count, never deflate it. A `PERMANENTLY_DEAD` verdict is therefore untouched by this.
Only the pass side is at risk.
