The Gap-Fade Shortfall

A cross-sectional intraday equity edge that is statistically real, replicates out of sample, and still cannot be traded — because it is smaller than the spread it would have to cross.

Verdict

Twenty-five of twenty-six pre-registered formulas are noise. The twenty-sixth — fade the overnight gap, cross-sectionally, in the last half hour of the session — clears every statistical gate this repo can put in front of it, and then dies on execution cost. It earns 0.90 basis points per unit of turnover out of sample. Crossing the spread costs 1.13.

The candidate

PA_GAP_CD_FADE

Score is the negated overnight gap divided by its own trailing 60-day standard deviation, clipped at ±5, across 34 mega-caps. The signal is fully known at 09:30. Entry is the VWAP of the 15:35–15:40 bar; exit is unconditional at the VWAP of the 15:55–16:00 bar. Dollar-neutral, L1 gross 1.0, flat at every close — so borrow cost is zero exactly.

Three kills

Every escape route was tested and closed

  • 01 The rescuing rail can only be priced where the signal is absent

    The pre-registered exit pays a full spread. The obvious fix is to exit in the closing auction, which costs no spread at all — but the 5-minute cube carries no official close. Run on the panel that does, restricted to the same 34 names, gross is positive across all three windows and eleven years: +2.60 / +1.23 / +1.45. Net at 1.0 bp is still negative in two of three, and the one positive window is the oldest, when spreads were wider — the wrong direction for an execution story.

    That table is an upper bound in any case. The ten-minutes-later control shows 87% of the held-out return arriving in the ten minutes right after a single-print entry — precisely the bounce-prone object a 5-minute VWAP fill damps out.

  • 02 The survivorship-free version is zero

    On the point-in-time top-500 panel — real historical membership, no survivorship bias, raw prices, official auction exit — the same gap fade scores +0.28 in 2016–20, +0.85 in 2021–24, and −0.02 out of sample. The breadth version drifts to zero and the design window is its outlier.

  • 03 The mechanism story does not hold out

    The positive-at-every-slot profile was the strongest argument that this is a mechanism rather than a slot, so it was tested directly. The identical score entered at 10:00 instead of 15:35 scores +1.47 gross on design and −0.47 held out. A sign flip across windows is noise, not mechanism. The late-day slot replicated; the all-day drift meant to explain it did not.

A degeneracy worth reusing. Because every book is cross-sectionally demeaned before normalising, subtracting the cross-sectional mean of a score is a no-op — so two pre-registered pairs turned out to be numerically identical formulas. The 26 formulas are 24 distinct books. In a dollar-neutral sweep, "market-adjusted X" and "X" are the same formula, and pre-registering both inflates the apparent breadth of the search without inflating the bar it has to clear.

Both nulls

What the bar was, and how it was built

The operative bar is a stationary block bootstrap of the best-of-26, block length 5 sessions, 2,000 resamples, drawing the same day indices for every config so the sweep's internal correlation survives. The analytic approximation is reported only as a cross-check, and understated the real bar by 12% — consistent with the standing note that it always does.

At +2.36 the candidate sits above the p99, so its family-wise p-value is below 0.01. The second null — permuting the score within same-day ADV deciles, 500 draws, run on gross rather than net because running it on net is mis-specified — returns p = 0.0020 against a null mean of +0.89.

Harness self-test. A planted edge scores +41.01 and mirrors exactly to −41.01 under negation; a random score returns +0.58; and a deliberately planted one-bar artefact scores +0.75 on the primary fill and collapses to −0.98 under the gap fill. The gate can see the object it exists to catch.

What this closes

The theme is finished, and the margin is the thing to write down

The last untested cell of intraday equity microstructure on free bar data has now been tested. The prior round killed opening drive, close drive and opening range on gross; this round killed path shape, opening-print dislocation and vol-scaled drive on gross too. The one remaining cell turned out to be statistically real and economically unreachable. Nothing further should be spent here.

That distinction matters. The prior round died before a single basis point of cost was charged — zero of 28 beat the gross bar. This round produced an effect that clears a family-wise p < 0.01, survives a permutation at p = 0.002, decomposes cleanly away from its entry print, and replicates out of sample with a larger daily return than it had on design — and then dies at 0.90 against 1.13. That is the execution-cost wall in its purest observed form, and the sharpest confirmation yet of the standing lesson that free data prices a statistic, not a claim.

Nothing is promoted. The basket forward clock is locked and this is not added to it. It is logged as a gross-real / net-dead observation — the correct object to re-test first, and only, if the execution assumption itself changes: measured price improvement on mega-cap marketable orders, a mid-point rail, or paid data.
