# New information candidates: who pays, what changes, and how to reject them

Author: Codex. Date: 2026-09-08 UTC. Status: **working hypotheses, not profitable strategies**.
Owner request: repair delayed capture and think beyond the repository's failed families.
No failed price/flow/exit grid was rerun. No published trading recipe was imported.
Novelty here means a materially different hypothesis from the inspected experiments;
worldwide originality is not established by a repository search.

## Decision rule for inventing

A candidate must identify (1) the information not already in the tested tape,
(2) why someone would transact differently after learning it, (3) why our receipt
can precede that incremental response after costs, and (4) a first test that can
kill it without optimizing returns. Negative unconditional drift is not a proof of
universal impossibility. It is also not overcome by more combinations, a larger
model, an elaborate stop, or confidence that an edge must exist.

**Verified current boundary:** the work so far repaired measurement and implemented
an observation-only birth-origin detector. It has not produced executable returns,
positive median PnL, or a profitable money-bot. Eight elapsed hours of capture will
not establish any of those claims. All old strategy retirements remain in force.

## Trading hypothesis A: canonical-address resolution

**Working hypothesis:** when several tokens compete to represent the same cultural
object, a previously established source can resolve *which exact token* its audience
should recognize. That change can direct incremental attention to one existing mint.
The proposed information is a newly authenticated address binding or correction—not
price momentum, social sentiment, the popularity of a theme, or a paid boost count.

Concrete event: a pre-registered artist/project/source identity previously had no
unique token binding (or a conflicting/incorrect one), then publishes a verifiable
record naming exactly one existing mint. For example, a signed repository record or
an author-controlled canonical page changes its token pointer. An exact address is
required; tickers and a token's own claim to be official are insufficient. Control
of an account/domain is evidence of control, not proof of honesty, artistic ownership,
economic independence or uncompromised keys.

**Proposed recipient:** the existing, explicitly identified mint. It is not chosen
as the largest coin, the best historical performer, the first listing, or whichever
later survived. Earlier lookalikes stay in the denominator and comparison universe,
including dead/unquoteable ones. The ANON/BRO anecdotes are excluded completely.

**Who pays if this works:** later voluntary buyers learning the identity resolution.
The hypothesis fails if market participants already act on that resolution before
we can observe, authenticate and enter, or if recognition never creates incremental
executable demand. There is no external cash entitlement or guaranteed price floor.

### Measurement instrument required before a financial preregistration

1. Freeze the source-identity universe without reference to token returns. Establish
   public source history before token promotion; do not select sources by their later
   successful coins. Keep sources that produce no token event in coverage accounting.
2. Retain original public records, content hashes/commit identifiers, receipt clocks,
   edits/deletions, key/handle changes and authentication results. Never substitute
   today's profile or mutable URL contents for what existed at the decision time.
3. Bind the exact chain and mint and its canonical creation evidence. Capture ALL
   candidates, refusals and conflicting bindings; never admit only those later priced
   by an aggregator. Metadata URLs alone are creator-controlled, not independent proof.
4. Availability is the time by which the record AND its required identity proof were
   received and verified. Publication timestamps and later historical reconstruction
   cannot be backdated into an actionable observation.
5. Treat public posts and metadata as untrusted data. Do not execute instructions in
   them or fetch arbitrary private-network URLs. No private information, covert audience
   tracking, paid coordination, promotion, or manufactured endorsement is involved.

**Primary-source feasibility, accessed 2026-09-08:** the
[AT Protocol DID specification](https://atproto.com/specs/did) provides public identity
and signing-key machinery. The maintained
[Bluesky Jetstream repository](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream) describes
archive/replay/streaming infrastructure and directs users to current documentation.
These establish possible instrumentation paths only. They do not prove retrospective
completeness, trustworthiness, enough relevant events, economic independence or alpha.
No social collector, account, subscription, or trading integration was activated here.

### Preregistered first-kill questions, not yet a money test

- Does a fixed prospective source universe produce genuine, previously ambiguous
  address-resolution events at all? If not, retire for insufficient opportunity; do
  not enlarge the source list based on observed winners.
- Does exact authentication finish before the relevant incremental market response?
  Record actual receipt/verification/route clocks. If not, retire for lateness. Merely
  preceding the next candle is insufficient evidence of executable lead.
- Is the apparent effect equally present for impostor sources, unchanged/repeated
  bindings, or address/time assignments shuffled within the relevant source/session
  dependence blocks? If so, there is no demonstrated identity-resolution mechanism.
- Are supposedly independent sources controlled by the same creator/funding network,
  or do they mainly announce their own already-bought bundle? Known links count against
  independence; missing links are unknown, not proof of independence.

Freeze finite observation duration, source selection, event definition and thresholds
BEFORE collecting the outcome cohort. This document is a hypothesis/measurement brief,
not a completed financial preregistration or permission to tune on development data.

Only after feasibility passes should a separately frozen money test apply the complete
owner harness: next real open after actual availability and feasible execution; one
position per mint; fixed pre-entry size; mint-equal means AND medians; every denominator
and horizon resolution rate; -30/-50/-100% missing-exit scenarios; no minimum bar/trade/
listing survival filter; all costs and jumps; mint/session bootstrap and creator/wallet/
funding leakage controls; whole-pipeline profitability placebo; selection multiplicity;
untouched multiweek confirmation. Include zero-entry weeks, not only active winners.
The 4.01pp historical session-noise figure is context, not a fee or a universal error bar.

**Decision:** this is the next distinct trading-information hypothesis worth a cheap
feasibility test. It is not yet evidence that any entry should be bought. Do not claim
the proposed mechanism escapes the toll until actual executable outcomes demonstrate it.

## Adjacent route B: earn from verified execution savings

**Alternative objective, not trading alpha:** a transparent execution-cost verification
or routing service could earn revenue from reducing other traders' costs, without
holding directional meme inventory. This is explicitly a business-model alternative
if the owner prioritizes income *around memecoins* over a proprietary trading bot.
It is not presented as a newly discovered or globally novel trading edge.

Using only the owner's supplied HISTORICAL figures, 3.6–4.2% terminal round trips
minus 1.14% direct routing leave an arithmetic 2.46–3.06 percentage-point difference.
That is potential cost headroom, not current executable savings, revenue or profit.
Route quality, notional, latency, venue/version, failed transactions and operational
costs may consume it. The counterfactual fills must be comparable; a cheaper quote
that cannot land or exit is not a saving.

**First-kill test:** paper-only matched-time, exact-notional execution-cost receipts.
Could a disclosed fixed service fee leave customers better off on comparable fills,
after RPC, transaction failures, support/security and acquisition costs? If not, kill
the economics. Real customer demand is a separate test; simulator savings do not prove
anyone will pay. No signing/treasury system belongs in this research service.

**Decision:** no pivot or product launch is authorized by this note. Customer-facing
execution would require a separate user decision, security design and applicable legal
review. It cannot be reported as success on the requested proprietary trading objective.

## Ideas rejected at the concept stage, NOT new empirical kills

- Stronger rug screens, smarter stops, another wallet-flow blend or another theme rank:
  already tested information families; no new independent information is identified.
- Shorting every eventual loser: loss knowledge arrives later, and executable borrowing
  or an appropriate contemporaneous hedge cannot be assumed. Existing perp failures
  are not reopened by knowing that spot coins eventually die.
- Selling rug insurance: premiums/claims/capital have not been measured; correlated
  jump losses and adverse selection do not disappear by switching sides of the payoff.
- Redeemable/escrow-backed memes as an arbitrage: no actual immutable external claim
  has been demonstrated, and generic redemption arbitrage would not meet the request
  for a genuinely new strategy. A token's marketing promise is not a redemption right.
- More compute as the mechanism: compute can search, authenticate and reject errors;
  it does not create a paying counterparty or a positive conditional expectation.

**Open question:** does hypothesis A produce a repeatable all-cost edge under the
full harness? No result exists yet. Consistent income is an empirical objective, not
something this document or an upgraded recorder can guarantee.
