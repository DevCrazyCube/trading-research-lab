## 4. What survives — as measurements, not signals

No claim survives as SIGNAL. Two survive as cheap, falsifiable measurements that the repo does not yet have; the leaderboard denominator is a third. Each carries a built-in placebo.

1. Creation-slot manufacture exclusion (from claim 3, exclusion half only).
   - Measure: for every pump.fun/LaunchLab mint, one getTransaction on the create tx: creator token delta / supply; migration in the creation slot (y/n); pool reserve / supply; largest single buy in the creation slot; count of equal-amount outbound creator transfers in 5 min; creator funding source shared with another creator in 7 days. Free (public RPC; ~2 calls per mint). Non-Solana rails need a per-factory parser (4/7 runners) — out of scope for a first pass.
   - Expected base rate: from the census, ~60% of >= $500k prints (1,078/1,784) are creation-slot completes and would be excluded; 0/7 runners would be excluded (USELESS is borderline: 9.64% flip, 10-s curve, but float default and no same-slot self-buy).
   - Placebo: apply the same filter to the survivorship-free census and compare 24 h / 72 h returns of the residual population against the unfiltered one under the repo's frozen handling rules. The claim to beat is "residual expectancy is still negative (best -30%)". If the residual is not better than a random same-size exclusion, the filter is hygiene only and must not be reported as edge. Also log evasion: the NTDA treasury already splits across ~2,000 sub-wallets; a dev-buy split across 3 wallets defeats the threshold.

2. Rail/quote universe ratio (from claim 1, reduced to a descriptor).
   - Measure: at pair creation record launchpad program/factory id, days since that program's first-seen launch, chain age, quote mint in {SOL, WSOL, WETH, USDC, USDT, WBNB} (y/n). Maintain a first-seen table (one row per program). Free.
   - Expected base rate: P(>= $10M | StonkFun launch) ~ 0.03% (8/25,914); pump.fun organic-cross P(>= $10M) UNKNOWN but of the 132 observed from-below crossers, 40% held $500k and the >= $10M count was not computed. The useful output is the ratio, expected near 1 given both are ~0.
   - Placebo: shuffle the rail label across mints of the same week and recompute; the rail effect must exceed the shuffled distribution's 95th percentile in two consecutive weeks. Report on the full denominator (all launches on the rail, not the top-50 page).

3. Board-wallet early-cluster conditional run rate (from claim 6, denominator only).
   - Measure: subscribe to the FOMO trade feed (or scan the top-150 wallets' ATAs daily before rent-closure); for every token they touch record age and mcap at first touch, count of board wallets within 90 min, and whether the token later prints $500k / $10M or dies. Free but rate-limited (public RPC; the earlier check used ~60 calls for 12 mints).
   - Expected base rate: ~1/8 run to >= $10M given >= 2 board wallets in 90 min, from the biased 12-mint sample; the unbiased rate is lower (closed ATAs on dead coins are invisible in that sample).
   - Placebo: the same statistic for a random set of 9 non-board wallets with matched activity (txs/day), and the repo's frozen 1-s-lag copy-trade harness (-4.5% to -12%) as the return benchmark. Do not report the cluster as a signal unless P(run) on the full denominator beats the matched random wallets and the copy-trade return turns positive after the 1.14% toll.

Not measurable for free and therefore dropped: sub-$100k minute paths of organic graduates (claim 4 needs a candle feed below the collector floor), pre-cross Telegram/X calls (claim 5), historical liquidity/holder counts at the crosses for 5/7 runners, and dev-buy fraction on non-Solana rails.

## 5. What this cannot show

- N = 7 runners, 1 control, one week of selection (plus USELESS from May 2025). Seven is enough to refute "all runners share X and dead coins lack it"; it is not enough to establish any positive rate. Every "4/7" or "5/7" here is inside the noise of coin-flip sampling.
- The control is a manufactured print, not an organic dead graduate. Contrasts against it separate fake from real, never dead from runner. Organic base rates came from the census (pump.fun only, >= $100k floor, 09-03..09-08) and the live StonkFun API (current-state, top-50 page + totals); neither covers Robinhood Chain, BSC/Flap, or Long.xyz, where 3/7 runners live.
- Hindsight sources: catalysts were assembled after the fact from press (KuCoin, Odaily, Phemex, hokanews, single-source listing dates), fxtwitter snowflake decoding, and profile notes; "first public mention" is "earliest found", not "earliest existing" — x.com returned 402/429, Telegram is invisible, and WebSearch negatives are weak. Several timings are inferred (CoinGecko listing from image cache-busters, ZEC payout start from a KOL's arithmetic, Vlad follow from secondary reports).
- Cross timestamps use price x 1e9 and a mix of first-wick / first-close / hourly definitions; burn-adjusted alternates shift PONS and ZCAT by 24-150 min. Liquidity at the crosses is measured for MarsCoin, MEME, KNOTS and modeled for PONS, USELESS, STONK (UNKNOWN), ZCAT (UNKNOWN). - Leaderboard first-touch times see only each wallet's current token account (2025 positions and rent-closed dead positions are invisible), the 7d board is selected on these very runners, and MarsCoin/USELESS board timings are UNKNOWN.
- FOMO /devs and /stats returned 503/400 on nearly every profile; dev/insider holdings at the crosses are UNKNOWN for all 7.
- Nothing here tests a trading rule. The repo's frozen results (unconditional entry negative under every handling rule; boosted coins lose at every horizon; copy-trade -4.5% to -12%; toll 1.14%; death is a -90% jump) remain the operative facts; this pass found no observable that would justify re-running them.
